What the service covers and how it works

The exact scope should be confirmed before you make plans. Describe the items and the area they’re in, then ask about access, preparation and scheduling. If removal is connected to a move, explain your moving dates too, especially if you’re coordinating packing or storage.

Key facts to gather:
- A clear list or description of the items to be removed.
- Where the items are located and any access details.
- Your preferred service date and any move-related deadlines.
- Whether you also need packing, moving or storage support.

Timing depends on scheduling, the amount of material, access and the work involved. Cost can vary with the scope, item volume, access and any related services. Ask how the provider determines the cost for your request, and make sure you understand what the arrangement includes before confirming.

A few terms help keep the conversation clear. Junk removal means taking away items you no longer want, subject to the provider’s accepted scope. Access describes how workers reach the items. Storage means keeping belongings in a facility rather than removing them.
