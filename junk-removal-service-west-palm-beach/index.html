What the service covers and how to compare options

Start by sorting belongings into three groups: keep, store, and remove. Then share the removal list and the property details with the provider. Clarify whether the service includes carrying items from their current location, hauling them away, and any disposal arrangements. Get the agreed scope and timing in writing.

When comparing providers, look beyond the headline service name. Check that each quote covers the same items and tasks. Ask about access needs, excluded materials, schedule changes, and what happens if the item list changes. A lower figure may not describe the same work as another quote, so compare the details rather than just the total.

A few terms you may hear:

- Access: the route to the items, including doors, stairs, and other obstacles.
- Scope: the agreed list of items and tasks for the service.
